Privacy Policy & Patient Confidentiality

At Muhammad Clinic, protecting your personal health information and privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our clinic or interact with our digital platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal and medical details strictly necessary for your treatment and appointment bookings, including:

  • Patient name, phone number, and contact details.
  • Medical history, consultation notes, and prescription records.
  • Appointment booking timestamps and inquiries made via WhatsApp or phone.

2. Confidentiality of Medical Records

All medical records, diagnoses, and treatments are handled with strict medical confidentiality. Your health data is only accessed by your attending physician or authorized clinic personnel and is never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.

3. Website Usage Data

Our website is hosted securely via GitHub Pages. We do not track or store intrusive personal data through our static informational website pages.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact us directly at Muhammad Clinic, Township, Lahore.
